## Build an appeal map

Analyze a reference at an abstract level:

| Dimension | Extract | Do not carry over directly |
|---|---|---|
| Function | confession, dance, chase, elegy, montage, suspense | the same branded story or scene |
| Energy | density curve, silence, release pattern | identical section timestamps |
| Rhythm | pocket, swing, subdivision, syncopation | a signature beat pattern |
| Harmony | brightness, tension, pedal behavior, rate of change | recognizable progression or voicing sequence |
| Melody | range, density, repetition mechanics | contour, intervals, cadence, or hook |
| Ensemble | acoustic/electronic balance and role exchange | the same signature instrument stack and roles |
| Vocal | register, distance, attack, group mechanics | voice imitation, phrasing, or ad-libs |
| Form | refrain-led, episodic, through-composed | identical section order and payoff placement |
| Production | dry/wide, live/programmed, rough/polished | distinctive source effects or mix fingerprint |

If the user names an artist, preserve only broad traits such as `close-miked low register`, `late pocket`, `sparse modal harmony`, or `chorus by subtraction`. Do not place the artist name in the provider prompt.

## Create an originality delta

Change at least five of these dimensions; for a famous or immediately recognizable reference, change at least seven:

1. premise and title
2. hook wording and phonetic shape
3. melodic contour and cadence
4. harmonic engine
5. groove, subdivision, or meter feel
6. section order and lengths
7. lead instrument and counter-voice
8. signature sound event
9. recording space and stereo movement
10. emotional resolution

Do not promise that this process guarantees non-infringement. It is a creative-distance method, not legal clearance or audio comparison.

## Write the creative thesis

Use:

```text
A <function> about <specific premise>, moving from <state A> to <state B>,
identified by <audible signature rule>.
```

Strong signature rules describe relationships:

- bass carries the hook while the voice avoids it
- cymbals appear only in the final chorus
- the chorus becomes smaller rather than larger
- a dry household sound becomes the percussion identity
- lead and counterline never play together until the ending
- each refrain removes one beat from the turnaround

Avoid `unique`, `cinematic`, `epic`, or `emotional` without an audible mechanism.

## Design the musical engine

Specify audible decisions in this order:

1. lane and emotional color
2. tempo, meter feel, and pocket
3. harmonic behavior
4. hook or motif mechanics
5. instrument roles and techniques
6. vocal register, distance, and delivery
7. section additions and removals
8. space, dynamics, and mix behavior
9. one signature constraint

Prefer relationships to inventory. Write `muted guitar answers the last word of each verse line` instead of `guitar`. Write `drums vanish for the first half of the bridge` instead of `dynamic drums`.

## Write vocal songs

### Establish the premise

Give the narrator:

- a concrete situation
- a desire
- an obstacle or contradiction
- a decision or consequence

Use physical evidence, actions, places, objects, and behavior. Replace default abstraction clusters—fire, shadows, echoes, scars, destiny, rising, breaking free—unless the brief makes one of them specific and necessary.

Make the title do conceptual work rather than merely restating the mood.

### Build the hook

- Make it understandable after one listen.
- Use a distinct rhythmic or syntactic shape.
- Favor open vowels for likely long notes.
- Repeat deliberately, then change a word or implication.
- Keep the promise emotionally legible without explaining the entire verse.
- Do not reproduce a reference hook's syllable count, rhyme positions, contour, or call-and-response pattern.

### Check prosody

- Speak every line aloud.
- Align natural stress with likely musical accents.
- Shorten lines that require rushed delivery.
- Put breath points at meaningful line breaks.
- Keep parallel lines compatible in stress without forcing identical syllable counts.
- Use perfect rhyme for closure and slant rhyme or assonance for unresolved feeling.
- Delete filler inserted only to complete a rhyme.

### Make sections earn their place

- Verse 2 must add information, escalate, or reverse perspective.
- A pre-chorus must tighten or redirect; omit it if it merely restates the verse.
- A bridge must create real contrast in point of view, harmony, rhythm, register, or arrangement.
- A final chorus should change delivery or context even when its words repeat.
- An outro should resolve, fracture, reprise, or intentionally withhold—not merely fade because the plan ran out.

## Write instrumental and picture cues

Replace lyrical argument with dramatic states:

1. establish a motif or texture
2. introduce motion, desire, or threat
3. create contrast through subtraction
4. reserve the densest or clearest passage for the visual pivot
5. create clean edit points, dropouts, or tails
6. resolve, fracture, or deliberately withhold resolution

For picture:

- identify exact emotional and editorial turns before choosing form
- place strong transients only where cuts or impacts can use them
- protect dialogue with sparse midrange or reduced density
- plan an edit-friendly hero passage rather than compressing a conventional full song into a tiny clip
- generate a longer coherent cue when practical, then cut the strongest passage to picture

Useful non-default forms include through-composed episodes, AABA, refrain at the end of each verse, instrumental hook instead of a second chorus, bridge by subtraction, shortened final refrain, and a false ending followed by one dry motif.

## Generate genuinely distinct directions

For a batch, vary all six anchors:

| Anchor | Contrasting examples |
|---|---|
| Premise | confession, bargain, ritual, misdirection, physical task |
| Hook | chant, long-vowel line, clipped question, instrumental motif, response chain |
| Groove | late pocket, motorik pulse, swung sixteenths, half-time march, free pulse |
| Harmony | pedal tension, modal vamp, descending bass, shifting dyads, chromatic planing |
| Lead identity | breath, bowed metal, prepared piano, plucked strings, found percussion |
| Form | refrain-led, through-composed, AABA, false ending, chorus by subtraction |

Reject batches that vary only the title, imagery, color, vocal gender, or a single instrument.

## Diagnose the song rather than the prose

| Symptom | Likely cause | Revision |
|---|---|---|
| Clean but generic | default form, groove, or harmonic loop | change hook carrier, form, or harmonic engine |
| Big all the time | additive arrangement | specify subtraction, silence, and narrow-to-wide movement |
| Lyrics feel artificial | abstraction, filler, forced rhyme | add physical evidence, decision, and natural stress |
| Chorus does not lift | no contrast or hook economy | strip the pre-chorus, simplify hook, change register or width |
| Vocal feels synthetic | overloaded technique or unclear identity | simplify timbre/delivery and reduce effects |
| Instruments feel fake | exposed generated timbre | mask with ensemble role, or replace/layer the defining part outside the model |
| Prompt is ignored | overload or contradiction | retain one primary lane, one contrast, and fewer audible rules |
| One weak section | local composition failure | repair that section's job and transition rather than restarting every field |
| Polished but forgettable | no signature mechanism | impose one motif, sound, or structural rule |

Choose composition and performance before polish. A remaster cannot create a missing hook, contrast, or premise.

## Quality gate

Confirm that:

- premise, title, hook, and imagery are not paraphrases of a reference
- no artist or source-title name appears in provider fields
- every instrument has a role or lifecycle
- at least three distinct energy states exist in a full song plan
- the signature rule is audible
- natural word stress supports likely melody
- every batch direction uses a different musical engine
- exact controls are labeled by runtime surface
- no uniqueness, rights, language, or fidelity claim exceeds the evidence
